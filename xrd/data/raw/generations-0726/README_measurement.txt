For XRD measurement:

All perovskite films were deposited on ITO substrates;

Perovskite#control: Perovskite film was deposited on top of ITO substrate;

Perovskite#Passivator 1: Perovskite film was deposited on top of ITO substrate, then passivator 1 was spin coated on top of perovskite;

Perovskite#Passivator 2: Perovskite film was deposited on top of ITO substrate, then passivator 2 was spin coated on top of perovskite;

Perovskite#Passivator 3: Perovskite film was deposited on top of ITO substrate, then passivator 3 was spin coated on top of perovskite;

Perovskite#Passivator 4: Perovskite film was deposited on top of ITO substrate, then passivator 4 was spin coated on top of perovskite;

Perovskite#Passivator 5: Perovskite film was deposited on top of ITO substrate, then passivator 5 was spin coated on top of perovskite;
