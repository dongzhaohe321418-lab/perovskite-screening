# WP3-V1: is the fine-tuning collapse of the Sr binding energy a loss-weighting artefact? (pre-specification)

Written 2026-11-15, before any V1 training step or V1 value exists. Post hoc with respect to WP3: the WP3 test set has
been used, so V1 is a diagnostic of the WP3 recipe, not an adoption test. No model from V1 is adopted for S2-S4 on
this evidence; adoption would need fresh hosts.

## 1. Question and hypothesis

WP3 (`results/objective2/dopant_shell/WP3_RESULT.md`) fine-tuned MACE-MP-0 medium on 201 DFT frames with
w_E = 70 000 and w_F = 100. The force RMSE on the 46 m30/m32 test frames fell from 86.81 to 14.94 meV/Å, but the
far-referenced E_b RMS over the 5 non-far m30 sites rose from 28.41 to 69.38 meV, driven by Sr (Sr RMS 9.20 -> 88.71
meV; GA 43.49 -> 15.16 meV) [wp3_eval.json criterion6.values].

WP3_RESULT §8 reason 1: the w_E rule of amendment m item 2 balanced the loss terms over all 130 energy-labelled
frames, of which the 24 vacancy-free MD/rattle frames carry 99.7 % of L_E (multi-eV zero-shot residuals). w_E came out
about 95 times lower than the 6.7 × 10⁶ that the same rule gives over the 106 band and binding frames alone.

**H1.** With w_E = 6.7 × 10⁶ (everything else identical), the Sr far-referenced E_b does not collapse.

## 3. Gates before any V1 value is read

- **G-train (local reproduction of the WP3 recipe).** A local 2-epoch run of the unchanged WP3 command must give the
  Initial validation loss 0.33213836 within 1e-6 relative, and the epoch-0 validation loss 9.66622193 within 1 %
  (thread count changes summation order). On failure V1 is not trained locally; it goes to the node after FR.
- **G-eval (evaluator reproduction).** The local evaluator reads DFT labels from `test.xyz` (REF_energy_raw,
  REF_forces); it opens no QE output. On ZS and WP3 it must reproduce, from wp3_eval.json criterion6.values:
  (i) within 0.01 meV/Å, (ii) raw RMS and (iii) raw RMS (all, Sr, GA) within 0.05 meV. On failure, the committed
  `scripts/39` is used instead.

## 4. Outcomes (test set, raw F basis, as criterion 6)

- **P1 (primary):** Sr far-referenced E_b error RMS over the 3 non-far m30 Sr sites (hinge 136, flank 124, mid 52;
  far site 64).
- P2: (iii) over all 5 non-far m30 sites. P3: (i) force RMSE over the 46 m30/m32 frames. P4: (ii) dE_a RMS over the
  4 m30/m32 pairs.
- Reported only: per-site errors and signs; m21 site errors (validation host, used for selection, not independent).

## 5. Reading (fixed now)

- **Collapse removed by the energy weight:** V1 P1 ≤ 25 meV and V1 P3 < 86.81 meV/Å.
- **Collapse persists:** V1 P1 > 50 meV. Claim 4 then stands for two energy weights.
- **Partial:** anything else; V2 then decides.
- If V1 P3 ≥ 86.81 meV/Å, this is reported as a force-energy trade-off whatever P1 reads.

## 6. Limits stated in advance

One seed; 3 Sr sites on one host; the test set was used before. Seed variance is not measured; a difference smaller
than about 20 meV in P1 is not interpreted.
